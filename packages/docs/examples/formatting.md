# Formatting

Select cells and right-click: the formatting bar opens above the menu, with bold, italic, underline, fill and text
colors, alignment and clear formatting. Point at a palette color to see it on the cells; click to keep it, or click
elsewhere to leave the cells as they were. Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I and Ctrl+U work too, the menu's **Format** entry has the
same commands, and undo (Ctrl+Z) takes any of it back. A downloaded Excel file keeps the formatting.
