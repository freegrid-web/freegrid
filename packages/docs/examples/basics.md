# Basics

Edit cells by double-clicking, pressing <kbd>Enter</kbd> or just typing. Drag the small square at the
bottom-right of the selection (the fill handle) to copy values. Copy and paste works with Excel and Google Sheets.
Right-click for the context menu, click a column header to sort, and drag header borders to resize.
Undo and redo with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Y</kbd>.
