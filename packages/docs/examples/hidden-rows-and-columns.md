# Hidden rows and columns

Row 6 and the columns C and D start hidden: the headers skip their numbers and letters, and a double line marks
where they are. Click one of the small arrows beside it to show them, or select the rows (columns) on both sides of a gap and
choose **Show rows** in the context menu. Select rows or columns and choose **Hide rows** to hide them.
