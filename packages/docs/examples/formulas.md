# Formulas

Cells that start with `=` show the result of their formula. Change a price or a quantity and the totals follow.
Double-click a total to see its formula. Insert or remove rows from the context menu: the references move with
them. The formulas are calculated by [IronCalc](https://github.com/ironcalc/IronCalc), loaded on this page.
